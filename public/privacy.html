LoadScore Beta Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Current beta behavior

LoadScore stores truck settings, preferences, saved loads, feedback, notification history, and a randomly generated installation identifier locally in the browser or extension. Web and extension storage are separate. Clearing local storage may reset the identifier.

Optional website account

LoadScore contains an optional email magic-link account system that becomes available when the production Supabase project is configured. Supabase Auth processes the email address and authentication/session records needed to sign in. Account email and authentication tokens are excluded from PostHog analytics. Signing in does not upload local freight, truck settings, saved loads, comparisons, alerts, imports, or preferences, and signing out does not delete them. Authentication does not provide cloud freight storage or cross-device recovery.

Highlighted text and imports

The extension reads deliberately highlighted, visible page text only after the driver activates the feature. Pasted text and selected CSV files are processed locally. LoadScore does not automatically monitor load boards, access passwords or freight-site cookies, bypass logins, or automatically book freight.

Product analytics and feedback

Anonymous product analytics are enabled by default and may be turned off at any time using the Anonymous Product Analytics control in LoadScore. PostHog receives only explicit, allowlisted custom product-use events and a randomly generated installation identifier. These events help measure actions such as opening LoadScore, calculating or comparing a load, using a feature, or sharing a result.

LoadScore does not intentionally send origin, destination, exact lane, exact offered rate, broker name, broker phone, broker email, load reference, highlighted or pasted freight text, CSV freight contents, account email, authentication tokens, credentials, freight-site cookies, or personal driver information to PostHog. Strict event and property allowlists remove unapproved fields before transmission. PostHog person-profile processing is disabled for these events.

In the LoadScore PostHog project, client IP storage, session replay, general autocapture, heatmaps, web-vitals and dead-click autocapture, form/input capture, console capture, and network header/body capture are disabled. LoadScore does not load the PostHog browser SDK; it sends only its explicit custom events. Turning analytics off stops future centralized product events without disabling the calculator, account authentication, or essential local storage. Problem reports remain local unless the driver deliberately prepares and reviews an email.

Diagnostics, sharing, and notifications

Copyable diagnostics contain build versions, coarse browser category, random local ID, selected mode, feature/error category, parser category, and timestamp. They exclude account email and authentication data. Sharing occurs only after the driver chooses Copy or Share. Notifications are off by default and evaluate only loads already provided to LoadScore; they do not create automatic load-board monitoring.

Retention and contact

Local analytics is bounded to 200 events and local beta reports to 50. A stable installation identifier lets LoadScore measure repeat anonymous product use; clearing browser/site storage may reset it. An anonymous installation is not automatically a verified working driver. Account deletion/support procedures must be finalized before broad production account distribution. LoadScore does not sell personal information. Questions: rgm88@loadscore.app.
