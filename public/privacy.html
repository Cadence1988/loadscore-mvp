LoadScore Beta Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Current beta behavior

LoadScore stores truck settings, preferences, saved loads, feedback, notification history, and a random anonymous installation identifier locally in the browser or extension. Web and extension storage are separate. Clearing local storage may reset the identifier. There are no LoadScore cloud accounts in this release.

Highlighted text and imports

The extension reads deliberately highlighted, visible page text only after the driver activates the feature. Pasted text and selected CSV files are processed locally. LoadScore does not automatically monitor load boards, access passwords or cookies, bypass logins, or automatically book freight.

Analytics and feedback

Optional centralized analytics is disabled and no central provider is configured in the current deployment. LoadScore is configuration-ready for PostHog custom-event analytics, but sends centrally only after the founder configures a project and the driver opts in. If enabled, PostHog receives a random local installation ID, event name, timestamp, build/surface categories, and strictly allowlisted feature-use properties. It does not receive raw freight text, routes, exact rates, broker contacts, credentials, or personal details. PostHog may process request-level network information; the founder must disable IP capture in PostHog project settings, document retention, and keep this policy synchronized before activation. Users can turn anonymous analytics off in LoadScore. Problem reports remain local unless the driver deliberately prepares and reviews an email.

Diagnostics, sharing, and notifications

Copyable diagnostics contain build versions, coarse browser category, random local ID, selected mode, feature/error category, parser category, and timestamp. Sharing occurs only after the driver chooses Copy or Share. Notifications are off by default and evaluate only loads already provided to LoadScore; they do not create automatic load-board monitoring.

Retention and contact

Local analytics is bounded to 200 events and local beta reports to 50. Central retention must be documented before activation. Clearing site data or removing/resetting the extension removes corresponding local data; server-side deletion by anonymous ID may be limited without accounts. LoadScore does not sell personal information. Questions: rgm88@loadscore.app.
