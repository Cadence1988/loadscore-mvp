LoadScore Beta Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Current beta behavior

LoadScore stores truck settings, preferences, saved loads, feedback, notification history, and a random anonymous installation identifier locally in the browser or extension. Web and extension storage are separate. There are no LoadScore cloud accounts in this release.

Highlighted text and imports

The extension reads deliberately highlighted, visible page text only after the driver activates the feature. Pasted text and selected CSV files are processed locally. LoadScore does not automatically monitor load boards, access passwords or cookies, bypass logins, or automatically book freight.

Analytics and feedback

Optional centralized analytics is disabled and no central provider is configured. Bounded local product events use allowlisted categories and exclude raw freight text, routes, rates, broker contacts, credentials, and personal details. Problem reports remain local unless the driver deliberately prepares and reviews an email.

Diagnostics, sharing, and notifications

Copyable diagnostics contain build versions, coarse browser category, random local ID, selected mode, feature/error category, parser category, and timestamp. Sharing occurs only after the driver chooses Copy or Share. Notifications are off by default and evaluate only loads already provided to LoadScore; they do not create automatic load-board monitoring.

Retention and contact

Local analytics is bounded to 200 events and local beta reports to 50. Clearing site data or removing/resetting the extension removes corresponding local data. LoadScore does not sell personal information. Questions: rgm88@loadscore.app.
