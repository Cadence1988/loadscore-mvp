## Method boundaries

- The official AMS workbook is quarterly. FREE-5 preserves that resolution and does not manufacture monthly or weekly detail.
- Peak means a quarterly mean share at least one-half standard deviation above the 25% quarterly baseline; shoulder is above 25% but below peak; off is at least one-half standard deviation below baseline.
- Historical consistency measures how often a year's largest quarter matches the structural peak quarter: HIGH at 5/7 years, MEDIUM at 3/7, otherwise LOW.
- NASS usual harvest dates validate timing context only. Harvest is not truck availability, and the 2007/2010 vintages require caution.
- FAF annual agricultural volume and AMS seasonal movement overlap conceptually. `doubleCountingKey` identifies the structural base so a future model does not add both at full weight.
- Cross-border streams describe movements through Arizona or Texas gateways, not domestic production in the named city.
- No current-year deviation, equipment weight, seasonal score, route, mileage, or live freight claim is used in production.
