## Interpretation

- Tons are thousand tons; value is million constant 2022 dollars.
- Total flow is outbound plus inbound directional handled freight; intraregional flows contribute to both sides.
- Percentiles use all 134 FAF6 domestic regions, not only LoadScore research markets.
- Commodity diversity is based on one minus the Herfindahl concentration of combined inbound/outbound truck tonnage.
- Current Reload Scores appear only for comparison. The research composite is a review heuristic and never a replacement score.
- CFS GAS01 is supplementary shipper-survey origin context. It is not expected to equal FAF because FAF adds sectors and foreign-trade modeling.
- No seasonal, equipment, routing, mileage, live-load, or load-board signals are included.
