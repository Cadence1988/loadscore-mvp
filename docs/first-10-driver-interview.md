# First 10 Driver Post-Use Interview

Updated: 2026-08-12

Ask neutrally after the driver has used LoadScore:

1. What do you normally use to decide whether to take a load?
2. Did LoadScore affect any decision you made? How?
3. Which number or explanation did you look at first?
4. What part of the score explanation made sense or did not make sense?
5. In what situation, if any, would you use Preferred, Flexible, or Recovery?
6. Would pasted text or CSV import fit your real workflow? Why or why not?
7. What felt slow, confusing, or unnecessary?
8. What information did you expect but not find?
9. Would you choose to use this again tomorrow? Why?
10. Would you tell another driver about it? Why or why not?
11. What change, if any, would make LoadScore worth paying for?
12. What price would feel reasonable, too expensive, and suspiciously cheap?

Record actual words and negative evidence. Do not coach toward positive answers or count an interview as repeat use.
