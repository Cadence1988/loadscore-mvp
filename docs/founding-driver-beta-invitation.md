# Founding Driver Beta Invitation

Updated: 2026-08-12

## Invitation copy

LoadScore is looking for a small first group of owner-operators and working drivers to test a private beta. It helps compare a freight offer against real truck costs, deadhead, reload-market estimates, operating goals, and minimum acceptable rate. It is a decision and alert layer, not a load board, and it does not scrape private sites or bypass logins.

Founding Drivers are asked to use the web app on real decisions when practical, try the Chrome extension through trusted Developer Mode installation, and give honest feedback about incorrect results, confusing steps, missing information, and whether the tool actually changes a decision. Testers should never share load-board credentials or private broker information in feedback.

The beta is free and under active development. Results are estimates and should be reviewed before making a freight decision. Chrome Web Store approval has not been obtained; invited extension testers receive the unpacked-install guide. There is no paid subscription or promised lifetime plan in the current beta.

## Tester follow-up

1. Confirm the tester completed one real, complete evaluation.
2. Ask the neutral post-use interview questions in `first-10-driver-interview.md`.
3. Record negative evidence and workflow friction, not just compliments.
4. Ask whether the driver used LoadScore again on another day.
5. Ask for one referral only after the tester finds genuine value.

## Honest benefit statement

Early testers receive direct access to the founder, influence over the first-driver workflow, and early notice of later beta access. Future pricing, discounts, or subscriptions remain undecided until real usage validates value.
